**

# 8‑Day Tokyo Itinerary  
*(Assumes a 2‑night arrival in Narita/Kanagawa and a 2‑night departure. Adjust dates/times per your flight schedule.)*  

---

## Day 1 – Arrival & Downtown Orientation  
| Time | Activity | Details & Tips |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 14:00 | Arrive at Narita/Kanagawa → Hotel Check‑in | Use Narita Express (N‑EX) to Tokyo‑Station, transfer to JR Yamanote to your accommodation. |
| 15:30 | Walk to **Yoyogi Park** | 20‑min stroll from Shinjuku. Perfect for a relaxed first evening – look for street performers and local food stalls. |
| 17:30 | **Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko Statue** | Take the Yamanote Line to Shibuya. Cross the scramble at the iconic spot, then snap photos with the Hachiko statue. |
| 19:00 | Dinner in Shibuya | Try **Uobei** (speedy sushi) or **Nagi Shokudo** for a cozy bento experience. |
| 21:00 | Return to hotel | Yamanote Line → JR Line back to Shinjuku / Asakusa. |

---

## Day 2 – Traditional & Modern East  
| Time | Activity | Details & Tips |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 08:30 | **Tsukiji Outer Market** | Arrive early to avoid crowds. Sample fresh sushi at **Sushi Dai** (reserve a seat or get a line number), grilled seafood at **Matsunabe Taku**. |
| 10:30 | **Senso‑ji Temple (Asakusa)** | Walk through Kaminarimon Gate → Nakamise‑Shopping Street → main hall. Bring a small donation (~¥200). |
| 12:30 | Lunch in Asakusa | Eat tempura at **Daikokuya** or try a kaiseki set at **Kagetsu**. |
| 14:00 | **Tokyo Skytree** | From Asakusa, walk to Asakusa Station → take the Tobu Skytree Line to Oshiage. Entry to the Tembo Deck: ¥2,800 (adult). Consider the *Skytree City* underground mall for souvenir shopping. |
| 17:00 | **Sumida River Cruise** | Board at Skytree Pier. 60‑min cruise offering views of the city, bridges, and the Museum of the Imperial Collections. |
| 19:00 | Dinner near Skytree | Try **Daikokuya Ramen** or **Sushi Iwa** for a riverside experience. |
| 20:30 | Return to hotel | Take the Toei Oedo Line (Skytree → Shinjuku). |

## Day 3 – Shibuya & Harajuku  
| Time | Activity | Details & Tips |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 09:00 | **Meiji Shrine** | Walk from Harajuku Station via Yoyogi Park. Pack a small snack; visitors may bring “otoshi‑cho” (offering). |
| 11:00 | **Yoyogi Park** | Stroll, join a dance class or watch a local band (especially weekends). |
| 12:30 | Lunch in Omotesandō | Trendy cafés like **Cafe de Lune** or **Harajuku Gyoza Lou**. |
| 14:00 | **Omotesandō & Takeshita Street** | Browse boutiques, vintage shops, and crepe stalls. |
| 16:00 | **Shibuya Scramble & Hachiko** | Return to Shibuya; enjoy the neon lights. |
| 18:00 | Dinner in Shibuya | Try **Uobei** or **Gonpachi** (famous for its “Kill Bill” vibe). |
| 20:00 | Optional: **Shibuya Sky Observation Deck** | Night view of Shibuya & Tokyo. Tickets ¥3,000. |

## Day 6 – Imperial & Roppongi  
| Time | Activity | Details & Tips |
|------|----------|----------------|

| 14:00 | Lunch in Ginza | **Sushi Saito** (reserves) or **Ippudo** for ramen. |
| 16:00 | **Roppongi Hills Mori Tower** | Visit Mori Art Museum (tickets ¥1,800), then enjoy the observation deck (¥2,400). |

| 20:00 | Dinner in Roppongi | Try **Narisawa** (reservation required) or **Gyukatsu** for a hearty meal. |

### General Tips  
- **Transport:** Get a Suica or Pasmo prepaid card; convenient for trains, buses, and most shops.  
- **Tickets:** Book major attractions (Skytree, TeamLab Borderless, Kabuki‑za, Mori Art Museum) online 1–2 weeks in advance to secure seats and discounts.  
- **Weather:** Pack an umbrella; Tokyo is rainy in spring/summer.  
- **Language:** A small phrasebook or translation app helps; many signs are bilingual.  
- **Dining:** Reserve at high‑end restaurants (Sushi Saito, Narisawa) at least a month ahead.  

Enjoy your immersive journey through Tokyo’s historic temples, futuristic museums, bustling markets, and serene parks!
