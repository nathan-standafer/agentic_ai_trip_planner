**Tokyo (February 26th - March 1st, 5 days)**

*   **Day 1 (Feb 26th):** Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration (4-6 hours). Check into hotel. Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (potentially early cherry blossoms). Dinner in Shinjuku's entertainment district. *Extra: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observation deck.*
*   **Day 2 (Feb 27th):** Shibuya & Harajuku (4-6 hours).  Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko statue.  Explore Harajuku's Takeshita Street and Meiji Jingu Shrine. *Extra: Cat Cafe.*
*   **Day 3 (Feb 28th):** Asakusa & Tokyo Skytree (4-6 hours).  Senso-ji Temple. Nakamise-dori street.  Tokyo Skytree for panoramic views. *Extra: Sumida River Cruise.*
*   **Day 4 (Mar 1st):**  Imperial Palace & Ginza (4-6 hours). Imperial Palace East Garden. Ginza district for shopping and dining. *Extra: TeamLab Borderless Digital Art Museum (book tickets in advance!).*

**Kyoto (March 2nd - March 4th, 3 days)**

*   **Day 5 (Mar 2nd):** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove & Tenryu-ji Temple (4-6 hours).  Arashiyama Bamboo Grove walk. Visit Tenryu-ji Temple. *Extra: Sagano Scenic Railway.*
*   **Day 6 (Mar 3rd):** Fushimi Inari Shrine & Kiyomizu-dera Temple (4-6 hours).  Fushimi Inari-taisha Shrine (thousands of red torii gates). Kiyomizu-dera Temple. *Extra: Nishiki Market (Kyoto’s kitchen).*
*   **Day 7 (Mar 4th):** Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) & Ryoan-ji Temple (Rock Garden) (3-5 hours).  Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion). Ryoan-ji Temple's Zen garden. *Extra: Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion).*

**Osaka (March 5th - March 7th, 3 days)**

*   **Day 8 (Mar 5th):** Osaka Castle & Dotonbori (4-6 hours). Osaka Castle. Explore Dotonbori’s vibrant nightlife and street food. *Extra: Osaka Museum of History.*
*   **Day 9 (Mar 6th):** Universal Studios Japan (Full Day). Enjoy theme park rides and attractions. *Extra: Consider express passes to minimize wait times.*
*   **Day 10 (Mar 7th):** Shinsekai & Departure (3-5 hours). Shinsekai district for a retro atmosphere. Tsutenkaku Tower. Depart from Osaka (Kansai International Airport - KIX). *Extra: Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan.*
