**Family Trip to Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka - 2026**

**Travelers:** Nathan (51), Laura (50), Lydia (17)
**Dates:** February 26 – March 7, 2026
**Flights:**
*   CMH to HND: February 25, 10:20 AM – February 26, 5:45 PM (Delta DL0275)
*   HND to CMH: March 7, 4:15 PM – March 7, 11:00 PM (Delta DL0274)
**Hotel:** Karaksa Hotel Tokyo Station (February 26 – March 3)
**Kyoto/Osaka Hotel:** To be booked separately (March 3 – March 7)

**Day 1: February 26 (Thursday) – Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration**

*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at HND. Transfer to Karaksa Hotel Tokyo Station. Settle in and leave luggage.
*   **Afternoon/Evening:** Head to Shinjuku. Visit the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for panoramic city views (Free). Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden for a relaxing escape.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Shinjuku. Consider Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) for a unique dining experience.

**Day 2: February 27 (Friday) – Akihabara & Anime/Manga Immersion**

*   **Morning:** Explore Akihabara – Electric Town. Visit Book-Off for used manga/anime goods, and browse electronics and gaming stores.
*   **Afternoon:**  Don Quijote Akihabara – a multi-story discount store for souvenirs and fun finds. Consider a themed cafe experience.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Akihabara.  Explore local arcades and game centers.

**Day 3: February 28 (Saturday) – Shibuya & Meiji Shrine**

*   **Morning:** Visit Meiji Jingu Shrine, a peaceful oasis dedicated to Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken.
*   **Afternoon:** Shibuya Crossing and Hachiko statue. Explore the trendy shops of Shibuya 109.
*   **Evening:** Shibuya Sky observation deck for stunning evening views of the city (book tickets in advance). Dinner in Shibuya.

**Day 4: March 1 (Sunday) – Tokyo Marathon & Creative Activity**

*   **Morning:** Nathan runs the Tokyo Marathon. Laura and Lydia can explore Ueno Park, visit the Tokyo National Museum, or relax. Utilize the marathon spectator information to find good viewing spots.
*   **Afternoon:** After Nathan finishes, Laura and Lydia can participate in a coin jewelry-making workshop. Search for workshops online and book in advance (consider areas like Harajuku or Shibuya).
*   **Evening:** Reunion dinner – celebrate Nathan’s accomplishment!

**Day 5: March 2 (Monday) – Ghibli Museum & Relaxation**

*   **Morning:** Ghibli Museum in Mitaka (Tickets *must* be purchased well in advance – they are extremely competitive).
*   **Afternoon:**  Relaxation time, explore a local neighborhood.  Consider visiting a cat cafe if Lydia is interested.
*   **Evening:**  Dinner near Karaksa Hotel or in a neighborhood of their choice.

**Day 6: March 3 (Tuesday) – Travel to Kyoto/Osaka & Initial Exploration**

*   **Morning:** Check out of Karaksa Hotel. Take the Shinkansen (bullet train) from Tokyo Station to Kyoto/Osaka.  (Book tickets in advance).
*   **Afternoon:** Check into Kyoto/Osaka hotel. Explore the immediate area.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Kyoto/Osaka. Consider trying a local specialty like Okonomiyaki.

**Day 7: March 4 (Wednesday) – Nara Deer Park & Osaka Delights**

*   **Morning:** Day trip to Nara. Visit Nara Deer Park and Todai-ji Temple, home to a giant bronze Buddha statue. Interact with the friendly wild deer.
*   **Afternoon:** Return to Osaka. Explore Dotonbori for street food and lively atmosphere.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Dotonbori.

**Day 8: March 5 (Thursday) – Osaka Shopping & Tsutenkaku Tower**

*   **Morning:** Shopping at Second Street Osaka for trendy clothing and accessories.
*   **Afternoon:** Visit Tsutenkaku Tower and slide down the iconic slipper slide. Explore Shinsekai.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Osaka, trying local ramen or Kushikatsu.

**Day 9: March 6 (Friday) – Muji & Mega Donki Exploration**

*   **Morning:** Visit a Muji shopping location for minimalist lifestyle goods.
*   **Afternoon:** Explore a Mega Donki store for a massive variety of goods from electronics to food.
*   **Evening:** Farewell dinner in Kyoto/Osaka, reflecting on the trip.

**Day 10: March 7 (Saturday) – Departure**

*   **Morning:** Final souvenir shopping, or revisit a favorite spot.
*   **Afternoon:** Travel from Kyoto/Osaka to HND airport.
*   **Evening:** Depart from HND to CMH (Delta DL0274).

**Notes:**

*   **Japan Rail Pass:** Consider purchasing a Japan Rail Pass if significant travel outside of Tokyo is planned.
*   **Pocket Wifi:** Rent a pocket wifi device for easy internet access.
*   **Suica/Pasmo Card:** Purchase a Suica or Pasmo card for convenient transportation on trains and buses.
*   **Reservations:**  Ghibli Museum tickets and popular restaurants require advance reservations.
*   **Flexibility:** This itinerary is a suggestion, feel free to adjust based on the family's preferences and energy levels.
