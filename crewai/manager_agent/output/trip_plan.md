# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**

*   **Flights:** Columbus (CMH) to Tokyo (HND) - Feb 25, 2026; Tokyo (HND) to Columbus (CMH) - March 7, 2026 (already purchased).
*   **Shinkansen (Bullet Train):** Tokyo to Kyoto - March 3, 2026 (Hikari or Kodama service). Estimated travel time: 2 hours 15 minutes to 3 hours. Cost: Approximately ¥13,000 - ¥15,000 per person (reserved seat). A Japan Rail Pass *might* be cost-effective depending on other travel plans.
*   **Shinkansen:** Kyoto to Osaka - March 3, 2026 (Hikari or Kodama service). Estimated travel time: 15-30 minutes. Cost: Approximately ¥3,000 - ¥5,000 per person (reserved seat).
*   **Local Trains:** JR lines (Yamanote, Keihin-Tohoku), subways (Tokyo Metro, Toei), Hankyu Railway (Kyoto to Osaka). Suica or Pasmo card recommended for easy payment.
*   **Buses:** Limited use, primarily for airport transfers or specific destinations.
*   **Taxis:** Available, but expensive. Primarily for short distances or when public transport is inconvenient.
*   **Ride-Sharing:** Uber is available in some areas, but not as prevalent as in the US.

**Hotel Accommodations:**

*   **Karaksa Hotel Tokyo Station:** 1-3-7, Yaesu, Chuo-ku, Tokyo 104-0028, Japan. Check-in: Feb 26, 2026. Check-out: March 3, 2026.
*   **Hotel Granvia Kyoto:** Kyoto Station, Karasuma Chuo-guchi, Shimogyo-ku, Kyoto, Kyoto 600-8216, Japan. Check-in: March 3, 2026. Check-out: March 5, 2026.
*   **Hotel New Hankyu Osaka Bay Tower:** 1-1, Bay-Shinkawa, Chuo-ku, Osaka 542-0011, Japan. Check-in: March 5, 2026. Check-out: March 7, 2026.
