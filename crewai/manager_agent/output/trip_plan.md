# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**
- **Shinkansen (Bullet Train):** Tokyo to Kyoto, Kyoto to Osaka (essential for speed and efficiency).  Purchase Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned (evaluate cost-effectiveness based on itinerary).
- **Local Trains (JR Lines):**  Extensive use within Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka for navigating neighborhoods and connecting to Shinkansen stations.
- **Subway:** Efficient and affordable for navigating city centers. Consider a Suica or Pasmo card for easy payments.
- **Buses:**  Common in Kyoto, useful for reaching destinations not well-served by trains.
- **Airport Transfers:** Narita Express (N’EX) or Limousine Bus from Narita (NRT), and Nankai Airport Express (Rapi:t) or Limousine Bus to Kansai International Airport (KIX).

**Hotel Accommodations:**
- **Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo:** 2-8-1 Nishishinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 160-8330, Japan. Check-in: 2026-02-26, Check-out: 2026-02-29. (4 nights)
- **Granbell Hotel Kyoto Arashiyama:** 19-1, Saga Tenryuji, Ukyo-ku, Kyoto 616-8385, Japan. Check-in: 2026-03-01, Check-out: 2026-03-04. (3 nights)
- **Cross Hotel Osaka:** 1-6-18, Namba, Chuo-ku, Osaka 542-0076, Japan. Check-in: 2026-03-04, Check-out: 2026-03-06. (2 nights)
