- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** 1100 Rock And Roll Blvd, Downtown Cleveland
- **HTTP Link:** https://rockhall.com/
- **Type:** Museum/Cultural
- **Estimated Duration:** 3 hours
- **Cost:** $36.99 adults; $26.99 children (ages 6-12); under 6 free
- **Suitability:** Excellent for families; all ages welcome

### Activity 2: Cleveland Museum of Art
- **Name:** Cleveland Museum of Art
- **Description:** World-class art museum with over 61,000 artworks spanning ancient to contemporary periods. Features free admission with diverse exhibitions, guided tours, and hands-on art activities. Located in University Circle cultural district.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** University Circle, 11150 East Boulevard
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.clevelandart.org/
- **Type:** Museum/Cultural
- **Estimated Duration:** 2.5 hours
- **Cost:** FREE admission (donations welcome)
- **Suitability:** Perfect for all ages; family-friendly with free activities

- **Sublocation:** 2000 Sycamore St, The Flats
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.greaterclevelandaquarium.com/

- **Description:** Largest natural science museum in Ohio featuring dinosaur exhibits, planetarium shows, Egyptian mummies, and interactive displays. Educational and engaging for all ages with rotating exhibitions.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** 1 Wade Avenue, University Circle
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.cmnh.org/
- **Type:** Museum/Educational
- **Estimated Duration:** 2.5 hours

### Activity 5: Great Lakes Science Center
- **Name:** Great Lakes Science Center
- **Description:** Interactive science museum with hands-on exhibits exploring STEM concepts. Features the Cleveland Clinic DOME Theater with giant 6-story domed screen showing immersive films. Free general admission with ticket cost for DOME theater.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** 601 Erieside Avenue, Downtown Cleveland
- **HTTP Link:** https://greatscience.com/
- **Type:** Educational/Entertainment
- **Estimated Duration:** 2.5 hours
- **Cost:** Free admission to exhibits; DOME Theater $10.95 adults, $8.95 youth (ages 2-12)
- **Suitability:** Perfect for curious kids and families interested in science

### Activity 6: West Side Market
- **Name:** West Side Market
- **Description:** Historic public market built in 1912, Cleveland's largest and oldest continuously operating market. Features 70+ vendors offering fresh produce, ethnic foods, prepared meals, bakery items, and specialty goods. Ideal for shopping and casual dining experience.
- **Suggested Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** 1979 West 25th Street, Ohio City
- **HTTP Link:** https://westsidemarket.org/
- **Type:** Shopping/Dining/Local Experience
- **Estimated Duration:** 1.5 hours
- **Cost:** Free entry; individual vendor prices vary ($5-20 per item)
- **Suitability:** Great for families seeking authentic local food and cultural experience

- **Suggested Duration:** 2 hours (tour + casual meal)
- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** 2516 Market Avenue, Ohio City
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.greatlakesbrewing.com/
- **Type:** Brewery Tour/Dining Experience
- **Estimated Duration:** 2 hours
- **Cost:** Brewery tour approximately $18-25 per adult (children may attend but no beer samples); restaurant meals $12-25 per person
- **Suitability:** Good for families; adults enjoy brewery tour while children can enjoy restaurant and grounds

- **Name:** Goodtime III Sightseeing Cruise
- **Description:** Largest quadruple-deck luxury cruise ship on Great Lakes (1,000 passenger capacity). Two-hour narrated cruise along Cuyahoga River and Lake Erie with spectacular views of downtown Cleveland skyline, industrial history, and waterfront attractions.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-2.5 hours
- **Location:** Cleveland, OH
- **Sublocation:** The Flats Waterfront (departure from downtown Cleveland)
- **HTTP Link:** https://goodtimeiii.com/
- **Type:** Entertainment/Sightseeing Cruise
- **Estimated Duration:** 2 hours
- **Cost:** Approximately $30 per person (seasonal variations; tours run Memorial Day-Labor Day with limited schedule May/September)
- **Suitability:** Great family experience; suitable for all ages
