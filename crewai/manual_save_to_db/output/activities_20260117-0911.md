- **Suggested Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Location:** Tokyo
- **Sublocation:** Shibuya/Yoyogi
- **Http_link:** https://www.meijijingu.or.jp/en/

- **Estimated Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Cost:** Free for shrine, ¥500 for gardens, ¥1,000 for museum
- **Suitability:** Family-friendly, all ages, no reservations needed
