### Day 1 - February 13, 2026

**Activity 1: Cincinnati Museum Center at Union Terminal**
- **Name:** Cincinnati Museum Center at Union Terminal
- **Description:** An award-winning complex housing five museums including Cincinnati History Museum, Museum of Natural History & Science, Duke Energy Children's Museum, Holocaust and Humanity Center, and Robert D. Lindner Family OMNIMAX Theater. Features Cincinnati's iconic historic 1940 scale model covering over 2,000 square feet and diverse traveling exhibitions.
- **Suggested Duration:** 3-4 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Downtown Cincinnati - Union Terminal, 1301 Western Avenue
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.cincymuseum.org/
- **Type:** Cultural Site / Museum / Family Activity
- **Estimated Duration:** 3-4 hours
- **Cost:** $25-35
- **Suitability:** Family-friendly, downtown location, educational, ideal for seeing Cincinnati history and sites

**Activity 2: John A. Roebling Suspension Bridge Walk & Photography Tour**
- **Name:** John A. Roebling Suspension Bridge Walk & Photography Tour
- **Description:** Historic 154-year-old suspension bridge spanning the Ohio River between Cincinnati and Covington, Kentucky. Walk across the iconic bridge with separated pedestrian walkway for views of the downtown skyline and riverfront. Takes 5-10 minutes to cross. Bridge is open to pedestrians (currently undergoing restoration with vehicular traffic limited). Perfect for photography and experiencing Cincinnati's iconic landmark up close.
- **Suggested Duration:** 1-1.5 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Downtown - Spans from downtown Cincinnati to Covington, KY over Ohio River
- **HTTP Link:** https://roeblingbridge.org/
- **Type:** Historic Site / Scenic Walk / Photography Experience
- **Estimated Duration:** 1-1.5 hours
- **Cost:** FREE
- **Suitability:** Iconic Cincinnati experience, free activity, unique photo opportunities, suitable for all fitness levels, downtown accessible

**Activity 3: Smale Riverfront Park**
- **Name:** Smale Riverfront Park
- **Description:** 45-acre modern riverfront park connecting downtown Cincinnati to the Ohio River. Features giant swings with river views (Rosenberg Swings), Carol Ann's Carousel, interactive foot piano, water play features, imaginative play areas, pollinator gardens, and historical monuments including Black Brigade Monument and Marian Spencer Statue. Stunning views of the riverfront activity.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Downtown Cincinnati Riverfront - Between Great American Ball Park and Paul Brown Stadium, 246 W. Rivercenter Blvd
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/cincyparks/visit-a-park/find-a-parkfacility/smale-riverfront-park/
- **Type:** Park / Entertainment / Local Experience
- **Estimated Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Cost:** FREE (carousel tokens $1-2 each)
- **Suitability:** Downtown location, free to explore, unique photo opportunities, family-friendly, accessible for all ages

### Day 2 - February 14, 2026

**Activity 4: Findlay Market**
- **Name:** Findlay Market
- **Description:** Ohio's oldest continuously operated public market (est. 1855) located in historic Over-the-Rhine neighborhood. Features fresh produce, specialty foods, local merchants, artisanal goods, farmers market (weekends), and live entertainment. 19th-century architectural charm surrounds the market.
- **Suggested Duration:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Over-the-Rhine District - 1801 Race Street
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.findlaymarket.org/
- **Type:** Shopping / Local Experience / Cultural Site
- **Estimated Duration:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Cost:** FREE to visit; purchases vary ($10-30 typical)
- **Suitability:** Historic Cincinnati landmark, downtown adjacent, family-friendly, unique local shopping experience, diverse food/merchandise

**Activity 5: Over-the-Rhine Historic District Exploration**
- **Name:** Over-the-Rhine Historic District Exploration
- **Description:** Historic neighborhood (blocks from downtown) featuring 19th-century German Revival and Italianate architecture, specialty boutiques, vintage shops, art galleries, and diverse dining. Main Street and Vine Street offer the most popular shopping and dining. Recent revitalization has made it one of Cincinnati's most affluent and desirable neighborhoods.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Over-the-Rhine District - Main Street & Vine Street
- **HTTP Link:** https://downtowncincinnati.com/districts/over-the-rhine/
- **Type:** Shopping / Cultural Site / Local Experience
- **Estimated Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Cost:** FREE to explore; shopping/dining costs vary ($15-50 typical)
- **Suitability:** Historic downtown location, family-friendly, shopping variety, dining options, unique local experience showcasing Cincinnati's cultural revitalization

**Activity 6: Cincinnati Streetcar Food Tour and Findlay Market Experience**

- **Type:** Food Tour / Entertainment / Culinary Experience / Streetcar Tour
- **Estimated Duration:** 3-4 hours
- **Cost:** $75-95 per person
- **Suitability:** Unique culinary experience, family outing, showcases local food scene, combines food with historic transportation and markets, suitable for adults and teen

### Day 3 - February 15, 2026

**Activity 7: American Sign Museum**
- **Name:** American Sign Museum
- **Description:** Unique museum displaying over 100 years of American sign history with more than 3,000 objects including vintage neon signs, hand-carved wooden signs, 1,500 photographs, 175 artworks, and 300 tools/equipment. Interactive exhibits showcase 'Main Street USA' recreations from 1870s-1970s era.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Nearby Downtown - 1330 Monmouth Avenue
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.americansignmuseum.org/
- **Type:** Entertainment / Unique Local Experience / Museum
- **Estimated Duration:** 2-2.5 hours
- **Cost:** $20
- **Suitability:** Family-friendly, unique Cincinnati experience, indoor activity suitable for winter February trip

**Activity 8: Cincinnati Art Museum**
- **Name:** Cincinnati Art Museum
- **Description:** World-class art museum featuring extensive collections spanning ancient to contemporary art. Free general admission with free exhibition hours on Thursday evenings 5-8pm. Features Art Climb open daily without tickets. Multiple galleries showcase paintings, sculptures, and decorative arts.
- **Suggested Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Downtown - 953 Eden Park Drive
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.cincinnatiartmuseum.org/
- **Type:** Cultural Site / Museum
- **Estimated Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Cost:** FREE general admission
- **Suitability:** Cultural experience, family-friendly, free option available, downtown accessible

**Activity 9: Taft Museum of Art (Weekend Free Option)**
- **Name:** Taft Museum of Art
- **Description:** National Historic Landmark housed in former home of President William Howard Taft's half-brother. Features world-class art collections and is Cincinnati's first Sensory Inclusive certified museum. Free admission on Sundays and Mondays. Features Taft Café and gift shop.
- **Suggested Duration:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Location:** Cincinnati, OH
- **Sublocation:** Downtown - 316 Pike Street
- **HTTP Link:** https://www.taftmuseum.org/
- **Type:** Cultural Site / Museum / Historic Site
- **Estimated Duration:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Cost:** FREE Sundays & Mondays; $15 adults, $12 seniors, Free youth 17 and under
- **Suitability:** Historic Cincinnati site, cultural experience, family-friendly, free on weekends, perfect for trip dates
