# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**
- Primarily utilizing Japan's efficient train system (JR Lines, Metro). Consider purchasing a Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned outside of Tokyo. Local buses will be used for shorter distances.
- Within cities: Walking and occasional taxi rides.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
- **Tokyo:** The Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo (2-2-1 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 160-8330) - Check-in: 2026-02-26, Check-out: 2026-02-27
- **Kyoto:** Hotel Granvia Kyoto (Karasuma-Dori Shiokoji-Sagaru, Shimogyo-Ku, Kyoto 600-8216) - Check-in: 2026-02-28, Check-out: 2026-03-01
- **Osaka:** Imperial Osaka Hotel (1-4-1 Shiokoji, Chuo Ward, Osaka 540-0015) - Check-in: 2026-03-02, Check-out: 2026-03-07
