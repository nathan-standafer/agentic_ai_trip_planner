# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:** Primarily JR Lines, local trains, subways, and buses. Consider purchasing a Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned. Suica/Pasmo card for local transport is highly recommended.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
- **Tokyo:** Park Hyatt Tokyo - 31F-7-2-2 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 163-0210, Japan. Check-in: 2026-02-26, Check-out: 2026-03-01.
- **Kyoto:** Hyatt Regency Kyoto - 644-2 Sanjusangendo-mawari, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto 605-0941, Japan. Check-in: 2026-03-02, Check-out: 2026-03-04.
- **Osaka:** Imperial Osaka Hotel - 1-8-35 Nakanoshima, Osaka 540-0071, Japan. Check-in: 2026-03-05, Check-out: 2026-03-07.
