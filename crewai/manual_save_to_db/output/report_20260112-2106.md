# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary

**Major Transportation:**
- **JR Train Pass:** Consider purchasing a JR East Pass for unlimited travel on most JR trains throughout the region
- **Tokyo Metro Pass:** 72-hour unlimited subway and train access (¥900)
- **Shinkansen:** High-speed rail from Tokyo to Kyoto and Osaka
- **Local Buses:** Available in each city for shorter transfers
- **Train Stations:** Tokyo Station (central hub), Kyoto Station, Osaka Station

**Hotel Accommodations:**
- **Tokyo:** Multiple hotel options near major stations (Shinjuku, Shibuya, Asakusa area) - February 26 to March 1
- **Kyoto:** Central Kyoto accommodation - March 2 to March 4
- **Osaka:** Central Osaka accommodation - March 5 to March 7
