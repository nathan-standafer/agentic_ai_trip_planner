## Trip Summary

This comprehensive 7-day itinerary for Great Smokey Mountains State Park features:

- **14 Total Activities** strategically distributed across 7 consecutive days
- **Hiking Mix:** 10 hiking trails ranging from easy 0.8-mile walks to challenging 8-mile adventures
- **Scenic Experiences:** 2 scenic driving experiences showcasing the park's most beautiful routes
- **Cultural Activities:** 1 day devoted to exploring Gatlinburg's vibrant downtown culture
- **Waterfall Features:** 6 hikes feature waterfall destinations (Laurel Falls, Cataract Falls, Chimney Tops, Abrams Falls, Oconalunga Falls, Ramsay Cascade)
- **Historic Elements:** Exploration of pioneer-era cabins and homesteads throughout multiple hikes
- **Elevation Gains:** Summit hikes include Mount LeConte (6,593 ft) and Bluff Mountain
- **Accessibility:** Trail difficulty balanced from easy to strenuous to accommodate varying fitness levels

**Total Trip Cost Estimate:**
- Park Admission: $30 (7-day vehicle pass)
- Accommodations: $600-1,200 (6 nights in Gatlinburg, $100-200 per night)
- Meals: $600-900 (estimated $30-45 per person daily)
- Activities/Attractions: $100-200 (Gatlinburg attractions are optional)
- Transportation/Fuel: $100-150
- **Total Estimated Cost: $1,430-2,480**

**What to Pack:**
- Sturdy hiking boots with good ankle support
- Moisture-wicking clothing and rain jacket
- Multiple layers (spring weather varies)
- Sunscreen and insect repellent
- Plenty of water bottles (hydration is critical)
- Camera for wildlife and landscape photography
- Day pack with snacks and first-aid supplies
- Binoculars for wildlife viewing
- Swimsuit (if interested in water activities)

**Best Practices:**
- Start hikes early to avoid crowds
- Check trail conditions before departure
- Be bear-aware (proper food storage and distance maintenance)
- Respect wildlife and maintain safe distances
- Leave no trace - pack out all trash
- Stay on marked trails
- Bring sufficient water for each hike
- Check weather forecasts regularly
- Allow flexibility for unexpected trail closures

This itinerary provides an immersive experience showcasing the Great Smokey Mountains' diverse attractions—from iconic waterfalls and mountain summits to historic heritage sites and vibrant gateway communities. The balanced mix of strenuous hiking adventures, scenic driving experiences, and cultural exploration ensures a memorable vacation for travelers of all interests and fitness levels.
