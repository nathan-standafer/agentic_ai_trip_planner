## Day 1 - 2026-03-19 (Thursday)
**Theme:** Rock & Roll Heritage and Historic Markets

| Time | Activity | Details | Transportation | Duration | Cost |
|------|----------|---------|----------------|----------|------|
| 09:00 AM | Rock & Roll Hall of Fame | World-renowned museum celebrating the history and culture of rock and roll music. [West Side Market](https://www.westsidemarketcleveland.com/) | Drive to 1979 West 25th Street, Ohio City | 1-2 hours | $20-35 per person (casual dining) |

## Day 2 - 2026-03-20 (Friday)
**Theme:** Science, Nature, and Marine Life Exploration

| Time | Activity | Details | Transportation | Duration | Cost |
|------|----------|---------|----------------|----------|------|
| 09:00 AM | Greater Cleveland Aquarium | Interactive aquarium featuring 5,000 creatures including sharks, stingrays, and Great Lake fish. Features hands-on exhibits, touch pools, and an underwater viewing tunnel. Perfect family entertainment on the Flats waterfront. [Greater Cleveland Aquarium](https://www.clevelandaquarium.org/) | Drive to 2000 Sycamore St, The Flats | 2-2.5 hours | $20-25 per person |
| 12:00 PM | Cleveland Museum of Natural History | Largest natural science museum in Ohio featuring dinosaur exhibits, planetarium shows, Egyptian mummies, and interactive displays. Educational and engaging for all ages with rotating exhibitions. Planetarium shows available. [Cleveland Museum of Natural History](https://www.cmnh.org/) | Drive to 1 Wade Avenue, University Circle | 2-3 hours | $18-22 per person |
| 04:00 PM | Great Lakes Science Center | Interactive science museum with hands-on exhibits exploring STEM concepts. Features the Cleveland Clinic DOME Theater with giant 6-story domed screen showing immersive films. Free general admission with ticket cost for optional DOME theater. [Great Lakes Science Center](https://www.glsc.org/) | Drive to 601 Erieside Avenue, Downtown Cleveland | 2-3 hours | Free general admission; DOME Theater $8-12 additional |
