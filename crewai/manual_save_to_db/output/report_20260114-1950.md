## Trip Summary

This 4-day Cleveland vacation offers a perfect blend of cultural experiences, family-friendly attractions, and local cuisine. The itinerary showcases Cleveland's major attractions including the iconic Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, world-class museums in the University Circle cultural district, and unique experiences like the historic West Side Market and Goodtime III cruise.

**Highlights:**
- **Day 1:** Begin with rock and roll history and a scenic Great Lakes cruise
- **Day 2:** Immerse yourself in world-class museums and craft brewery culture
- **Day 3:** Enjoy interactive family experiences from zoo to science center
- **Day 4:** Wrap up with quirky movie history and peaceful botanical gardens

**Budget Estimates:**
- Attractions: $200-300 per day
- Food/Dining: $100-150 per day
- Total estimated trip cost: $1,200-1,800 (excluding hotel and transportation from home)

**Best Time to Visit:** May offers pleasant spring weather perfect for outdoor activities and the cruise season. All attractions are seasonal-appropriate with good weather conditions.
