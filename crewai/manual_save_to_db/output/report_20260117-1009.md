## Additional Trip Notes

**Weather Considerations:** January weather in Cincinnati typically ranges from 30-40°F. Pack layers, warm coat, comfortable walking shoes, and consider weather conditions when planning outdoor activities.

**Dining Recommendations:** Cincinnati is known for Cincinnati-style chili (Skyline or Gold Star Chili). Over-the-Rhine offers trendy dining options. OTR Brewing, Rhinegeist Brewery, and local coffee shops provide authentic Cincinnati experiences.

**Total Estimated Cost per Person:** $200-350+ (depending on dining choices, activity selections, and transportation method)

**Pro Tips:**
- Download the GO Cincinnati app for public transit information
- Many museums offer discounted rates for early morning or evening hours
- Comfortable walking shoes recommended as much of the trip involves walking
- Consider purchasing multi-museum passes for additional savings
- Parking available in downtown lots; validate at museums when possible
