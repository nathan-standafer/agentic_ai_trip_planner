| Time | Activity | Details | Transportation | Duration | Cost |

[Krohn Conservatory](https://www.cincinnatiparks.com/krohn-conservatory/) | Driving to Eden Park | 1.5-2 hours | Admission fees apply |
| 02:00 PM | Taft Museum of Art | National Historic Landmark housed in former home of President William Howard Taft's half-brother. Features world-class art collections. Perfect timing for Sunday visit with FREE admission today. Cincinnati's first Sensory Inclusive certified museum offering historic site with cultural experience. Located at 316 Pike Street. [Taft Museum of Art](https://www.taftmuseum.org) | Driving downtown | 1.5-2 hours | Free (Sundays & Mondays) |
