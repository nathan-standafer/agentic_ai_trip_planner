**SATURDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2026**

**09:00 AM - American Sign Museum** ⭐ *NEW ACTIVITY*
- Duration: 1.5-2 hours
- Location: Cincinnati, OH
- Transportation: Driving to 1330 Monmouth Avenue
- Description: Unique indoor museum featuring over 150 vintage neon and electric signs from Cincinnati's Golden Age of advertising and entertainment, showcasing mid-century Americana and iconic vintage signage
- Notes: Perfect indoor winter activity for February. Fascinating collection of restored neon signs with great photo opportunities. Family-friendly with nostalgic Americana appeal.

**11:00 AM - Over-the-Rhine Historic District Exploration**
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Location: Cincinnati, OH
- Transportation: Walking or short drive from downtown
- Description: Historic neighborhood featuring 19th-century German Revival and Italianate architecture, specialty boutiques, vintage shops, art galleries, and diverse dining
- Notes: Recently revitalized neighborhood one of Cincinnati's most desirable. Main Street and Vine Street offer popular shopping and dining.

**02:30 PM - Cincinnati Art Museum**
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Location: Cincinnati, OH
- Transportation: Driving to Eden Park area
- Description: World-class art museum featuring extensive collections spanning ancient to contemporary art with free general admission
- Notes: Free admission with free exhibition hours on Thursday evenings 5-8pm. Multiple galleries showcase paintings, sculptures, and decorative arts.

**SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 15, 2026**

**09:00 AM - Findlay Market**
- Duration: 1.5-2 hours
- Location: Cincinnati, OH
- Transportation: Walking or short drive to 1801 Race Street
- Description: Ohio's oldest continuously operated public market (est. 1855) located in historic Over-the-Rhine neighborhood featuring fresh produce, specialty foods, local merchants, and artisanal goods
- Notes: Historic Cincinnati landmark, downtown adjacent, family-friendly, diverse food and merchandise options. FREE to visit.

**11:30 AM - Krohn Conservatory - Cincinnati's Botanical Garden**
- Duration: 1.5-2 hours
- Location: Cincinnati, OH
- Transportation: Driving to Eden Park at 1501 Eden Park Drive
- Description: One of the largest public greenhouses featuring 3,500+ plant species across seven collections including Fern House, Palm House, Desert House, and Orchid House
- Notes: Indoor winter activity, perfect for February. Beautiful photography opportunities, family-friendly nature and plant education.

**02:00 PM - Taft Museum of Art**
- Duration: 1.5-2 hours
- Location: Cincinnati, OH
- Transportation: Driving downtown to 316 Pike Street
- Description: National Historic Landmark housed in former home of President William Howard Taft's half-brother. Features world-class art collections. Free admission on Sundays and Mondays.
- Notes: Perfect timing for Sunday visit - FREE admission today. Cincinnati's first Sensory Inclusive certified museum. Historic site with cultural experience.
