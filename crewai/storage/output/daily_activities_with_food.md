**Detailed Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka Trip (Feb 26 - Mar 7, 2026) - with Food Options**

**Day 1: Feb 26 (Tokyo - Arrival & Shinjuku)**
* **5:45 PM:** Arrive at Haneda Airport (HND).
* **6:30 PM:** Transfer to Karaksa Hotel Tokyo Station.
* **8:00 PM:** Evening: Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.
* **9:00 PM:** Dinner in Shinjuku: *Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane)* offers a nostalgic experience with small yakitori (grilled chicken skewers) and ramen shops. Alternatively, *New York Bar* at the Park Hyatt Tokyo offers a high-end experience with stunning city views. **Ginza Happo-shinjuku** is a potential option as well.

**Day 2: Feb 27 (Akihabara & Shibuya)**
* **9:00 AM:** Morning: Akihabara – Explore electronics stores, anime/manga shops, arcades.
* **12:00 PM:** Lunch in Akihabara: *Gyukatsu Motomura* for deep-fried breaded beef cutlets, or *Marugo* for delicious tempura.
* **1:30 PM:** Shibuya Crossing & Shopping.
* **6:00 PM:** Shibuya Sky.
* **8:00 PM:** Dinner in Shibuya: *Uobei Shibuya Dogenzaka* for conveyor belt sushi, or *Han no Daidokoro Bettei* for yakiniku (grilled meat).

**Day 3: Feb 28 (Meiji Shrine & Harajuku)**
* **9:00 AM:** Morning: Meiji Jingu Shrine.
* **11:00 AM:** Harajuku & Takeshita Street.
* **1:00 PM:** Lunch in Harajuku: **Harajuku Gyoza Ru** for gyoza (potstickers).  Alternatively, try the trendy crepe stands on Takeshita Street.
* **2:30 PM:** Shopping in Harajuku.
* **6:00 PM:** Dinner in Harajuku: *Afuri Harajuku* for yuzu ramen.

**Day 4: Mar 1 (Tokyo Marathon & Female Activities)**
* **7:00 AM:** Nathan – Travel to Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for the Tokyo Marathon.
* **9:10 AM:** Nathan – Tokyo Marathon start time.
* **9:00 AM:** Laura & Lydia – Option 1: Coin Jewelry Workshop. Option 2: **Ghibli Museum** (highly recommend booking well in advance). Option 3: Shopping at Don Quijote or Mega Donki.
* **1:00 PM:** Lunch – Nathan rejoins the family. Consider a casual place near the Marathon route.
* **3:00 PM:** Relax and recover.
* **7:00 PM:** Family Dinner: Consider exploring restaurants in the Ginza area, known for its high-end dining.

**Day 5: Mar 2 (Tokyo Skytree & Asakusa)**
* **10:00 AM:** Morning: Tokyo Skytree.
* **12:00 PM:** Lunch near Tokyo Skytree: Solamachi restaurant complex offers a wide variety of options.
* **1:30 PM:** Asakusa & Senso-ji Temple.
* **4:00 PM:** Shopping at Muji.
* **6:00 PM:** Dinner in Asakusa:  Try *Daikokuya Tempura* for a classic Asakusa experience.

**Day 6: Mar 3 (Travel to Kyoto/Osaka & Exploration)**
* **8:00 AM:** Check out from Karaksa Hotel Tokyo Station.
* **8:30 AM:** Travel to Tokyo Station.
* **9:30 AM:** Take the Shinkansen to Kyoto/Osaka.
* **12:00 PM:** Arrive in Kyoto/Osaka. Check in to new hotel.
* **2:00 PM:** Explore Dotonbori in Osaka.  Enjoy takoyaki (octopus balls), okonomiyaki (savory pancake), and kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers) from street vendors.
* **7:00 PM:** Dinner in Dotonbori: Continue exploring Dotonbori's street food or try a local restaurant.

**Day 7: Mar 4 (Nara Park & Deer)**
* **9:00 AM:** Travel to Nara Park.
* **10:00 AM:** Nara Park.
* **1:00 PM:** Lunch in Nara: Try *Kakinoha-zushi*, Nara's local sushi specialty wrapped in persimmon leaves.
* **3:00 PM:** Return to hotel in Osaka/Kyoto.
* **6:00 PM:** Dinner in Osaka/Kyoto.

**Day 8: Mar 5 (Osaka – Tsutenkaku & Shopping)**
* **9:00 AM:** Tsutenkaku Tower.
* **11:00 AM:** Shopping at Second Street.
* **1:00 PM:** Lunch near Tsutenkaku:  Explore the restaurants in Shinsekai, the area surrounding Tsutenkaku.
* **3:00 PM:** Explore Book-Off stores.
* **6:00 PM:** Dinner in Osaka: Try *Kushikatsu Daruma* for a classic Kushikatsu experience.

**Day 9: Mar 6 (Kyoto - Flexible Activities & Final Shopping)**
* **Morning:** Option 1: Fushimi Inari Shrine. Option 2: Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.
* **1:00 PM:** Lunch in Kyoto: Explore Nishiki Market for a variety of Kyoto specialties.
* **3:00 PM:** Final shopping at Mega Donki or other stores.
* **6:00 PM:** Farewell Dinner: A Kaiseki dinner (traditional multi-course Japanese dinner) would be a memorable farewell experience.

**Day 10: Mar 7 (Departure)**
* **9:00 AM:** Final packing & last-minute souvenir shopping.
* **11:00 AM:** Travel to Narita or Haneda Airport.
* **4:15 PM:** Depart from Narita/Haneda Airport.
* **11:00 PM:** Arrive in Columbus, OH.

**Notes on Specific Requests:**

*   **Ghibli Cafe:** Extremely difficult to access without advance booking. Research and attempt to secure tickets well in advance. Locations vary, check official Ghibli Museum website.
*   **Godaime Hanamaya Udon:** Located near Tokyo Station, convenient for Day 1 and Day 6.
*   **Pizza Marumo:** A popular Tokyo pizza restaurant – consider visiting on Day 2 or Day 3.
*   **Mufusand Cat Cafe:** Located in Shinjuku, an option for Day 1 or Day 4.
*   **Coin Jewelry Workshop:** Needs advance booking and research.
