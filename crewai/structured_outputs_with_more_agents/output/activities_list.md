Here's a potential vacation plan for a traveller to Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka between the dates of 2026-02-26 and 2026-03-07.

**Tokyo (February 26th - March 2nd)**

*   **Day 1 (Feb 26):** Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration - Check into hotel, explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden, dinner in Shinjuku's vibrant entertainment district.
*   **Day 2 (Feb 27):** Culture & Pop Culture - Visit Meiji Shrine, explore Harajuku (Takeshita Street), and immerse yourself in the anime/electronics haven of Akihabara.
*   **Day 3 (Feb 28):** TeamLab Borderless & Ginza - Experience the immersive art of TeamLab Borderless (book tickets in advance!), followed by high-end shopping and dining in Ginza.
*   **Day 4 (Mar 1):** Day Trip to Hakone - Scenic mountain resort with views of Mt. Fuji, hot springs, and art museums (consider the Hakone Free Pass).
*   **Day 5 (Mar 2):** Tokyo Skytree & Sumida River Cruise – Enjoy panoramic views of the city from the Tokyo Skytree, then relax with a Sumida River cruise. *Possible event: Check TokyoCheapo or Gotokyo for events happening in February.*

**Kyoto (March 3rd - March 5th)**

*   **Day 6 (Mar 3):** Fushimi Inari & Kiyomizu-dera - Hike through the thousands of red torii gates at Fushimi Inari Shrine, then visit Kiyomizu-dera Temple for stunning views. *Possible event: Setsubun Festival at Yasaka Shrine.*
*   **Day 7 (Mar 4):** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove & Tenryu-ji Temple - Immerse yourself in the beauty of the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and explore the serene Tenryu-ji Temple. Consider renting a kimono for a traditional experience.
*   **Day 8 (Mar 5):** Gion District & Nishiki Market - Wander through the historic Gion district, hoping to catch a glimpse of a geisha, and indulge in local delicacies at Nishiki Market. *Possible event: Check Magical Trip for Kyoto winter festivals.*

**Osaka (March 6th - March 7th)**

*   **Day 9 (Mar 6):** Osaka Castle & Dotonbori - Visit Osaka Castle, explore the surrounding park, and experience the vibrant nightlife and street food scene in Dotonbori.
*   **Day 10 (Mar 7):** Universal Studios Japan or Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan & Departure - Choose between thrilling rides at Universal Studios Japan or exploring the marine life at Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan. Depart from Osaka. *Possible event: Check Magical Trip for Osaka winter events.*

**Extra Activities (Flexibility)**

*   **Tokyo:** Mori Digital Art Museum, Shibuya Crossing, Yoyogi Park
*   **Kyoto:** Kinkakuji (Golden Pavilion), Ryoan-ji Temple (Zen Garden), Philosopher's Path
*   **Osaka:** Shinsekai district, Tennoji Zoo, Minoo Park (if time allows and weather permits).

**Note:**

*   February is winter in Japan. Pack warm clothing, including layers, a hat, gloves, and a scarf.
*   The weather can be unpredictable, so be prepared for rain or snow.
*   Purchase a Japan Rail Pass if you plan to travel extensively by bullet train. Consider regional passes for local travel.
*   Book accommodations, popular attractions (like TeamLab Borderless and Universal Studios Japan), and transportation in advance, especially during peak season.
*   Be aware of any specific events or festivals happening during your trip and adjust your itinerary accordingly.
*   Check official websites for up-to-date information on opening hours, admission fees, and event details.
