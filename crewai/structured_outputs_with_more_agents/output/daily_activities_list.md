**Vacation Itinerary: Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka (February 26th - March 7th, 2026)**

**Tokyo (February 26th - March 2nd)**

*   **February 26th (Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration):**
    *   Check into hotel.
    *   Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.
    *   Dinner in Shinjuku's entertainment district.
    *   *Possible event: Check TokyoCheapo or Gotokyo for events happening in February.*
*   **February 27th (Culture & Pop Culture):**
    *   Visit Meiji Shrine.
    *   Explore Harajuku (Takeshita Street).
    *   Immerse yourself in Akihabara (anime/electronics).
*   **February 28th (TeamLab Borderless & Ginza):**
    *   Experience TeamLab Borderless (book tickets in advance!).
    *   High-end shopping and dining in Ginza.
*   **March 1st (Day Trip to Hakone):**
    *   Scenic mountain resort with views of Mt. Fuji.
    *   Hot springs.
    *   Art museums (consider the Hakone Free Pass).
*   **March 2nd (Tokyo Skytree & Sumida River Cruise):**
    *   Panoramic views from Tokyo Skytree.
    *   Sumida River cruise.
    *   *Possible event: Check TokyoCheapo or Gotokyo for events happening in February.*
    *   *Flexibility: Mori Digital Art Museum, Shibuya Crossing, Yoyogi Park*

**Kyoto (March 3rd - March 5th)**

*   **March 3rd (Fushimi Inari & Kiyomizu-dera):**
    *   Hike through Fushimi Inari Shrine (thousands of torii gates).
    *   Visit Kiyomizu-dera Temple (stunning views).
    *   *Possible event: Setsubun Festival at Yasaka Shrine.*
*   **March 4th (Arashiyama Bamboo Grove & Tenryu-ji Temple):**
    *   Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.
    *   Explore Tenryu-ji Temple.
    *   Consider renting a kimono.
*   **March 5th (Gion District & Nishiki Market):**
    *   Wander through Gion district (chance to see a geisha).
    *   Indulge in local delicacies at Nishiki Market.
    *   *Possible event: Check Magical Trip for Kyoto winter festivals.*
    *   *Flexibility: Kinkakuji (Golden Pavilion), Ryoan-ji Temple (Zen Garden), Philosopher's Path*

**Osaka (March 6th - March 7th)**

*   **March 6th (Osaka Castle & Dotonbori):**
    *   Visit Osaka Castle.
    *   Explore Osaka Castle Park.
    *   Experience Dotonbori (nightlife & street food).
*   **March 7th (Universal Studios Japan or Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan & Departure):**
    *   Choose between:
        *   Universal Studios Japan (thrilling rides).
        *   Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan (marine life).
    *   Depart from Osaka.
    *   *Possible event: Check Magical Trip for Osaka winter events.*
    *   *Flexibility: Shinsekai district, Tennoji Zoo, Minoo Park (if time allows and weather permits).*

**Important Notes (Applicable to the entire trip):**

*   **Weather:** February is winter in Japan. Pack warm clothing, layers, hat, gloves, and scarf. Be prepared for rain or snow.
*   **Transportation:** Consider a Japan Rail Pass for extensive travel or regional passes for local travel.
*   **Bookings:** Book accommodations, popular attractions (TeamLab Borderless, Universal Studios Japan), and transportation in advance.
*   **Events:** Check official websites for up-to-date information on opening hours, fees, and events.
