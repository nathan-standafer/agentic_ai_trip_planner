**Vacation Itinerary: Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka (February 26 - March 7, 2026)**

**February 26, 2026: Tokyo - Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration**

*   Arrival in Tokyo (Airport Transfer & Hotel Check-in)
*   Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden
*   Evening: Local Ramen experience. **Food Option:** *Ramen Hosenka* (known for its unique flavor) or *Hayashida* (popular and Michelin-recognized). *Ore no Sora* is also recommended.

**February 27, 2026: Tokyo - Markets, History & Iconic Crossing**

*   Tsukiji Outer Market (Seafood Breakfast). **Food Options:** *Kaisendon* (seafood rice bowl), *Tamagoyaki* (rolled omelet), fresh sushi from a market stall. Consider *Cafe Tsumugi* for an 18-dish Buddhist-inspired breakfast. *Kitsuneya* for organ miso stew.
*   Imperial Palace East Garden
*   Shibuya Crossing (Experience the Scramble)
*   *Alternative:* TeamLab Borderless/Planets (Requires Advance Booking)

**February 28, 2026: Tokyo - Hakone Day Trip (Mount Fuji & Hot Springs)**

*   Day trip to Hakone (Travel & Exploration)
*   Mount Fuji Views (Weather Permitting)
*   Hot Springs Experience (Onsen)
*   Return to Tokyo in the Evening. **Food Option:** Consider trying local Hakone cuisine near your onsen or a quick ramen stop back in Shinjuku upon return.

**March 1, 2026: Tokyo - Tradition & Sumo**

*   Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple)
*   Nakamise-dori (Traditional Shopping Street). **Food Option:** Try traditional snacks like *age-manju* (deep-fried sweet bun) or *kaminari okoshi* (rice crackers).
*   Possible Sumo practice viewing (Check Schedule & Availability)
*   *Alternative:* Ghibli Museum (Requires Advance Booking). **Food Option:** If visiting Ghibli, look for a cafe nearby with Japanese comfort food.

**March 2, 2026: Kyoto - Travel & Gion District**

*   Travel to Kyoto via Shinkansen (Bullet Train) - *Book Shinkansen tickets in advance!*
*   Hotel Check-in in Kyoto
*   Explore Gion (Geisha District). **Food Option:** Enjoy a traditional *kaiseki* dinner (multi-course Japanese haute cuisine) in Gion for an upscale experience, or sample local Kyoto specialties like *yudofu* (boiled tofu) at a smaller restaurant.

**March 3, 2026: Kyoto - Shrines & Temples**

*   Fushimi Inari Shrine (Thousands of Torii Gates). **Food Option:** Try *Inari sushi* (sushi in fried tofu pouches), a local specialty named after the shrine.
*   Kiyomizu-dera Temple (Wooden Stage & Views). **Food Option:** Sample Kyoto-style pickles and sweets along the streets leading to the temple.
*   *Alternative:* Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion). **Food Option:** Explore the restaurants around Kinkaku-ji for a more relaxed meal.

**March 4, 2026: Kyoto - Plum Blossom Festivals**

*   Jonangu Weeping Plum and Camellia Festival (Feb 18–Mar 22, 2026)
*   Possible Umenomiya Taisha Plum Festival (March 1, 2026) – *Confirm dates closer to travel*
*   *Alternative:* Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. **Food Option:** Try *yuba* (tofu skin) dishes, a specialty of Arashiyama.

**March 5, 2026: Osaka - Travel & Castle Exploration**

*   Travel to Osaka via Shinkansen
*   Hotel Check-in in Osaka
*   Osaka Castle (Explore the Castle and Park). **Food Option:** Street food vendors near Osaka Castle often offer *takoyaki* (octopus balls) and other Osaka favorites.
*   Dotonbori (Evening Street Food & Entertainment). **Food Options:** *Takoyaki*, *okonomiyaki* (savory pancake), *kushikatsu* (deep-fried skewers).

**March 6, 2026: Osaka - History and Retro Vibes**

*   Miyoshi Nagayoshi Warrior Procession (March 1, 2026) – *Confirm date/time closer to travel*
*   Shinsekai (Retro District) – Tsutenkaku Tower. **Food Option:** Try *kushikatsu* in Shinsekai – it’s a local institution.
*   *Alternative:* Universal Studios Japan. **Food Option:** Standard theme park food, but look for unique Japanese-themed offerings.

**March 7, 2026: Osaka - Departure**

*   Departure from Osaka (Airport Transfer). **Food Option:** Grab a quick breakfast or *onigiri* (rice balls) at the airport before departure.
