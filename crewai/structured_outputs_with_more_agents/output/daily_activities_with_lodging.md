## Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka Lodging & Itinerary (February 2026)

Here's a preliminary itinerary and lodging suggestions, incorporating potential events and food options. This is a draft and can be further customized.

**Tokyo (7 Days)**

*   **Lodging:** Shinjuku area. Options include:
    *   **Budget:** APA Hotel Shinjuku Gyoemmae (~$80-$120/night) - Known for efficiency and convenience.
    *   **Mid-Range:** Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo (~$150-$250/night) – Offers great views and easy access to transportation.
    *   **Luxury:** Park Hyatt Tokyo (~$400+/night) - Iconic views (Lost in Translation fame).
*   **Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration**
    *   Arrive at Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). Transfer to Shinjuku hotel.
    *   Lunch: Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) - Yakitori and ramen.
    *   Afternoon: Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (seasonal plum blossoms).
    *   Dinner: Robot Restaurant (unique entertainment).
*   **Day 2: Culture & Views**
    *   Morning: Meiji Jingu Shrine (peaceful oasis).
    *   Lunch: Harajuku - trendy cafes and street food (crepes!).
    *   Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko statue.
    *   Evening: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for panoramic views.
    *   Dinner: Shibuya - diverse dining options.
*   **Day 3: Asakusa & Tokyo Skytree**
    *   Morning: Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise-dori (traditional shopping).
    *   Lunch: Asakusa - tempura or soba noodles.
    *   Afternoon: Tokyo Skytree (modern landmark).
    *   Dinner: Sumida River cruise with dinner (optional).
*   **Day 4: Day Trip – Hakone (Mountain Scenery & Art)**
    *   Bullet train to Hakone.
    *   Hakone Open-Air Museum.
    *   Lunch: Local restaurant in Hakone (try soba or udon).
    *   Lake Ashi cruise.
    *   Return to Shinjuku. Dinner in Shinjuku.
*   **Day 5: Akihabara & Ueno**
    *   Morning: Akihabara (electronics, anime).
    *   Lunch: Akihabara – themed cafes or curry.
    *   Afternoon: Ueno Park & Museums (Tokyo National Museum, Ueno Zoo).
    *   Dinner: Ueno - izakaya (Japanese pub).
*   **Day 6: Pokemon & Shopping**
     *   Morning: Visit the new Pokémon theme park near Tokyo (if it has events).
     *   Lunch: Local restaurant near the Pokemon park.
     *   Afternoon: Shopping in Ginza.
     *   Dinner: Ginza - upscale dining options.
*   **Day 7: Departure**
    *   Enjoy a final Japanese breakfast.
    *   Depart from Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND).

**Kyoto (4 Days)**

*   **Lodging:** Gion or near Kyoto Station.
    *   **Budget:** Piece Hostel Kyoto (~$30-$50/night)
    *   **Mid-Range:** Hotel Granvia Kyoto (~$120-$200/night) - Conveniently located in Kyoto Station.
    *   **Luxury:** Ritz-Carlton Kyoto (~$500+/night)
*   **Activities:** Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Kiyomizu-dera Temple. Food: Kaiseki (multi-course meal), matcha sweets.

**Osaka (3 Days)**

*   **Lodging:** Namba or Umeda area.
    *   **Budget:** J-Hoppers Osaka Universal (~$25-$40/night)
    *   **Mid-Range:** Cross Hotel Osaka (~$100-$180/night)
    *   **Luxury:** St. Regis Osaka (~$350+/night)
*   **Activities:** Osaka Castle, Dotonbori (street food), Universal Studios Japan (optional). Food: Takoyaki, okonomiyaki.

**February Considerations:**

*   **Plum Blossom Festivals:**  Look for events in late February, especially in Tokyo and Kyoto.
*   **Lunar New Year:** Might impact some businesses and transportation.
*   **Weather:** Expect cold temperatures – pack warm layers.
