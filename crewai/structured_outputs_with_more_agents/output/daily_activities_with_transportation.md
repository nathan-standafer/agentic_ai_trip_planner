## Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka Lodging & Itinerary (February 2026)

Here's a preliminary itinerary and lodging suggestions, incorporating potential events and food options. This is a draft and can be further customized.

**Tokyo (7 Days)**

*   **Lodging:** Shinjuku area. Options include:
    *   **Budget:** APA Hotel Shinjuku Gyoemmae (~$80-$120/night) - Known for efficiency and convenience.
    *   **Mid-Range:** Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo (~$150-$250/night) – Offers great views and easy access to transportation.
    *   **Luxury:** Park Hyatt Tokyo (~$400+/night) - Iconic views (Lost in Translation fame).
*   **Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku Exploration**
    *   Arrive at Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). Transfer to Shinjuku hotel.
        *   *Transportation Options:*
            *   **Narita (NRT) to Shinjuku:** Narita Express train (~90 minutes, ~$30), Limousine Bus (~90-120 minutes, ~$30), Taxi (~90-120 minutes, ~$200).  Narita Express is likely most cost-effective and efficient.
            *   **Haneda (HND) to Shinjuku:** Keikyu Line train to Shinagawa, then JR Yamanote Line to Shinjuku (~30-45 minutes, ~$5), Limousine Bus (~45-75 minutes, ~$15), Taxi (~30-45 minutes, ~$70). The Keikyu/Yamanote combination is the most budget-friendly.
    *   Lunch: Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) - Yakitori and ramen.
    *   Afternoon: Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (seasonal plum blossoms).
    *   Dinner: Robot Restaurant (unique entertainment).
*   **Day 2: Culture & Views**
    *   Morning: Meiji Jingu Shrine. *JR Yamanote Line from Shinjuku to Harajuku Station.*
    *   Lunch: Harajuku - trendy cafes and street food (crepes!).
    *   Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko statue. *JR Yamanote Line from Harajuku to Shibuya Station.*
    *   Evening: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for panoramic views. *Short walk from Shibuya Station.*
    *   Dinner: Shibuya - diverse dining options.
*   **Day 3: Asakusa & Tokyo Skytree**
    *   Morning: Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise-dori (traditional shopping). *JR Yamanote Line from Shinjuku to Kanda Station, then Ginza Line subway to Asakusa Station.*
    *   Lunch: Asakusa - tempura or soba noodles.
    *   Afternoon: Tokyo Skytree (modern landmark). *Tokyo Metro Ginza Line from Asakusa to Ueno, then Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line to Oshiage (Skytree) Station.*
    *   Dinner: Sumida River cruise with dinner (optional).
*   **Day 4: Day Trip – Hakone (Mountain Scenery & Art)**
    *   Bullet train to Hakone. *Shinjuku Station to Odawara Station (Hakone) via JR Tokaido Shinkansen (Hikari/Kodama).  ~35 minutes, ~$30-40 one way.  Purchase a Hakone Free Pass for transportation within Hakone.*
    *   Hakone Open-Air Museum. *Hakone Tozan Railway or bus.*
    *   Lunch: Local restaurant in Hakone (try soba or udon).
    *   Lake Ashi cruise. *Bus or train to Lake Ashi.*
    *   Return to Shinjuku. *Odawara Station to Shinjuku Station via JR Tokaido Shinkansen.* Dinner in Shinjuku.
*   **Day 5: Akihabara & Ueno**
    *   Morning: Akihabara (electronics, anime). *JR Yamanote Line from Shinjuku to Akihabara Station.*
    *   Lunch: Akihabara – themed cafes or curry.
    *   Afternoon: Ueno Park & Museums (Tokyo National Museum, Ueno Zoo). *JR Yamanote Line from Akihabara to Ueno Station.*
    *   Dinner: Ueno - izakaya (Japanese pub).
*   **Day 6: Pokémon & Shopping**
    *   Morning: Visit the new Pokémon theme park near Tokyo (PokéPark Kanto located in Yomiuriland). *JR Keiyo Line from Tokyo Station to Maihama Station (transfer point), then Yomiuriland Line to Yomiuriland Station (~45-60 minutes, ~$8).*
    *   Lunch: Local restaurant near the Pokémon park (Yomiuriland).
    *   Afternoon: Shopping in Ginza. *JR Yamanote Line from Yomiuriland Station to Yurakucho Station, then a short walk.*
    *   Dinner: Ginza - upscale dining options.
*   **Day 7: Departure**
    *   Enjoy a final Japanese breakfast.
    *   Depart from Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). *Reverse the arrival transportation options.*

**Kyoto (4 Days)**

*   **Lodging:** Gion or near Kyoto Station.
    *   **Budget:** Piece Hostel Kyoto (~$30-$50/night)
    *   **Mid-Range:** Hotel Granvia Kyoto (~$120-$200/night) - Conveniently located in Kyoto Station.
    *   **Luxury:** Ritz-Carlton Kyoto (~$500+/night)
*   **Transportation from Tokyo to Kyoto:** Shinkansen (bullet train) from Tokyo Station or Shinagawa Station to Kyoto Station (~2.5-3 hours, ~$130-150).
*   **Day 1 (Kyoto):** Fushimi Inari Shrine. *JR Nara Line from Kyoto Station to Inari Station.*
*   **Day 2 (Kyoto):** Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion). *Kyoto City Bus from Kyoto Station (various routes available).*
*   **Day 3 (Kyoto):** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. *JR Sagano Line from Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama Station.*
*   **Day 4 (Kyoto):** Kiyomizu-dera Temple. *Kyoto City Bus from Kyoto Station.*

**Osaka (3 Days)**

*   **Lodging:** Namba or Umeda area.
    *   **Budget:** J-Hoppers Osaka Universal (~$25-$40/night)
    *   **Mid-Range:** Cross Hotel Osaka (~$100-$180/night)
    *   **Luxury:** St. Regis Osaka (~$350+/night)
*   **Transportation from Kyoto to Osaka:** Shinkansen (bullet train) from Kyoto Station to Shin-Osaka Station (~15 minutes, ~$15) or JR Kyoto Line to Osaka Station (~30 minutes, ~$5).  Local trains are cheaper.
*   **Day 1 (Osaka):** Osaka Castle. *Osaka Loop Line from Shin-Osaka or Osaka Station to Osakajokoen Station.*
*   **Day 2 (Osaka):** Dotonbori. *Midosuji Line from Shin-Osaka or Osaka Station to Namba Station.*
*   **Day 3 (Osaka):** Universal Studios Japan (Optional). *JR Yumesaki Line from Osaka Station to Universal City Station.*

**February Considerations:**

*   **Plum Blossom Festivals:**  Look for events in late February, especially in Tokyo and Kyoto.
*   **Lunar New Year:** Might impact some businesses and transportation.
*   **Weather:** Expect cold temperatures – pack warm layers.
