# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:** Shinkansen (bullet train) for intercity travel (Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka), JR lines and subways within cities, local buses. Consider purchasing a Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned. Hakone Free Pass for travel within the Hakone area. Suica or Pasmo card for easy payment on public transit.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
*   **Tokyo (Shinjuku):** Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo (2026-02-26 to 2026-03-02)
*   **Kyoto (Near Kyoto Station):** Hotel Granvia Kyoto (2026-03-02 to 2026-03-05)
*   **Osaka (Namba):** Cross Hotel Osaka (2026-03-05 to 2026-03-07)
