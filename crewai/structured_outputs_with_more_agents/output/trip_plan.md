# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**
- **Shinkansen (Bullet Train):** Tokyo to Kyoto, Kyoto to Osaka (Reserved seats recommended, purchase Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned)
- **Local Trains (JR Lines):** Primarily utilizing JR lines for intra-city travel in Tokyo and Osaka.
- **Subway (Tokyo Metro, Toei Subway):** Essential for navigating Tokyo's extensive underground network.
- **Buses:**  Utilized for Hakone day trip and local transport where trains are less convenient.
- **Taxi:** For shorter distances or when convenience is prioritized.
- **Airport Transfers:** Narita Express from Narita Airport (NRT) to Tokyo. Airport Limousine Bus from Kansai International Airport (KIX) to Osaka.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
- **Tokyo (February 26-March 1, 2026):** Park Hyatt Tokyo (Address: 3-7-1-2 Nishi Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 163-0558) - Check-in: 2026/02/26, Check-out: 2026/03/01
- **Kyoto (March 2-March 4, 2026):**  Hyatt Regency Kyoto (Address: 644-2 Sanjusangendo-mawari, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto 605-0941) - Check-in: 2026/03/02, Check-out: 2026/03/04
- **Osaka (March 5-March 7, 2026):** Imperial Hotel Osaka (Address: 1-8-38 Nakanoshima, Osaka 540-0071) - Check-in: 2026/03/05, Check-out: 2026/03/07
