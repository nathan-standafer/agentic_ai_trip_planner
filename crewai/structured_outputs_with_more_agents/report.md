# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**
-   **Shinkansen (Bullet Train):** Tokyo to Kyoto (and Kyoto to Osaka) - Purchase Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned, otherwise individual tickets.
-   **Tokyo Metro & JR Lines:** Extensive network for navigating Tokyo. Consider a Suica or Pasmo card for easy payment.
-   **Kyoto City Bus & Subway:** Primary method for getting around Kyoto. A day pass may be beneficial.
-   **Osaka Metro & JR Lines:** Efficient public transport in Osaka.
-   **Hakone Free Pass:** Essential for traveling around Hakone.
-   **Local Trains/Buses:** Used for shorter distances within cities.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
-   **Tokyo:** Park Hyatt Tokyo (Address: 3-7-1-2 Nishi Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 163-0558) – Check-in: 2026-02-26, Check-out: 2026-03-02
-   **Kyoto:** Hyatt Regency Kyoto (Address: 644-2 Sanjusangendo-mawari, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto 605-0941) – Check-in: 2026-03-03, Check-out: 2026-03-06
-   **Osaka:** The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka (Address: 1-5-1, Nakanoshima, Chuo-ku, Osaka 540-6036) – Check-in: 2026-03-07, Check-out: 2026-03-08
