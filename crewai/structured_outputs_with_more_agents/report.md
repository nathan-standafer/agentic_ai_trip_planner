# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**
- **Shinkansen (Bullet Train):** Tokyo to Kyoto (approx. 2.5 hours).  Consider purchasing a Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned. Advance reservations recommended, especially during peak seasons.
- **Local Trains (JR Lines):** Primarily using JR Yamanote Line in Tokyo, and local lines within Kyoto and Osaka. Suica/Pasmo card recommended for easy travel.
- **Subway:** Tokyo and Osaka Metro systems.
- **Buses:** Used in Hakone and for access to some attractions.
- **Taxi:** Available but expensive; best used sparingly.
- **Airport Transfers:** Narita Express or Limousine Bus from Narita; Keikyu Line or Tokyo Monorail from Haneda.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
- **Tokyo (Shinjuku):** Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo - 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-02 (7 nights) - Address: 2-2-1 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 160-8330, Japan. Includes breakfast.
- **Kyoto:** Hotel Granvia Kyoto - 2026-03-02 to 2026-03-06 (4 nights) - Address: Higashishiokoji Kamishichiken-dori, Shimogyo-ku, Kyoto 600-8216, Japan. Convenient location.
- **Osaka:** Cross Hotel Osaka - 2026-03-06 to 2026-03-07 (1 night) - Address: 1-7-18 Namba, Chuo-ku, Osaka 542-0076, Japan. Close to Dotonbori.
