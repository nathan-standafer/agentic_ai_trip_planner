# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:** This itinerary utilizes a combination of Japan’s extensive rail network (Shinkansen, JR lines, and local subways), buses, and taxis. A Japan Rail Pass is highly recommended for this trip to maximize cost-effectiveness. Individual tickets will be necessary for non-JR lines or occasional taxi rides. Local transportation cards (Suica or Pasmo) are essential for seamless travel on local trains and subways.
**Hotel Accommodations:**
