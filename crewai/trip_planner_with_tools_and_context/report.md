### Additional Tips & Practical Information  

1. **Transportation**  
   * **JR Pass** covers Shinkansen, local JR lines, and the **JR Haneda Airport Express**.  
   * For Tokyo metro, use **Suica/Pasmo**; for Kansai, **ICOCA**; in Kyoto, **Kitaca** works too.  
   * Reserve **Shinkansen** seats for early morning or late‑night times if you wish to avoid crowds.

2. **Accommodations**  
   * Book hotels with **free breakfast** if possible.  
   * For Kyoto, a **ryokan** (traditional inn) with an **onsen** experience on Day 5 or 8 adds cultural depth.

3. **Dining**  
   * Try **Sushi** at **Tsukiji Outer Market** early morning (best quality).  
   * Reserve a table at **Gion Karyo** (Kyoto) for a 3‑course kaiseki dinner.  
   * Explore **takoyaki** at **Dotonbori** and **okonomiyaki** at **Mizuno** (Osaka).

4. **Entrance Fees**  
   * **Tokyo Skytree**: ¥2,800 (Tower), ¥2,500 (Aquarium).  
   * **TeamLab Borderless**: ¥3,200.  
   * **Osaka Castle Museum**: ¥600.  
   * **Osaka Aquarium**: ¥2,400 (adult).  
   * **Universal Studios**: ¥7,800–¥9,500 (express pass extra).

5. **Best Times to Visit**  
   * **Fushimi Inari**: Dawn or dusk for fewer crowds.  
   * **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**: Early morning or late afternoon.  
   * **Osaka Castle Park**: Cherry‑blossom season (mid‑March to mid‑April).  
   * **Dotonbori**: Night for neon ambiance.

6. **Cultural Etiquette**  
   * Keep phones on silent in temples.  
   * Don’t tip; a polite “arigatou” suffices.  
   * Use both hands when exchanging business cards (if applicable).
