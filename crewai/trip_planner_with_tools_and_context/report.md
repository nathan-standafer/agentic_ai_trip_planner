# Trip Itinerary for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-07

## Summary
**Major Transportation:**
*   **Shinkansen (Bullet Train):** Tokyo to Kyoto (approx. 2.5 hours) and Kyoto to Osaka (approx. 30 minutes). Purchase Japan Rail Pass if extensive travel is planned.
*   **Local Trains (JR & Subway):** Primarily used within each city for shorter distances.  Suica/Pasmo card recommended for ease of payment.
*   **Buses:** Utilized for specific destinations or areas with limited train access.
*   **Taxi:** An option for convenience but more expensive.
*   **Walking:** Essential for exploring neighborhoods and attractions.

**Hotel Accommodations:**
*   **Tokyo:** Park Hyatt Tokyo (3376-2, Shibuyu, Nishi-Shibuya 2-36-21, Tokyo 150-0045) - Check-in: 2026-02-26, Check-out: 2026-03-01.  (Luxury option, excellent views)
*   **Kyoto:** Tawaraya Ryokan (Fuyacho Anekoji-agaru 285, Nakagyo-ku, Kyoto 604-8094) - Check-in: 2026-03-01, Check-out: 2026-03-04. (Traditional Ryokan experience)
*   **Osaka:** Imperial Osaka Hotel (1-1-1 Nakanoshima, Kita-ku, Osaka 530-0005) - Check-in: 2026-03-04, Check-out: 2026-03-07. (Convenient location)
