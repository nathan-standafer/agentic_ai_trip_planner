# 5-Day Paris Trip Plan

## Day 1: Arrival & Iconic Sights
- **Morning:** Begin with the **Louvre** to see masterpieces like the Mona Lisa, Winged Victory of Samothrace, and Venus de Milo.
- **Lunch:** Dine at **Café Marly** with views of the Louvre Pyramid or a nearby brasserie offering prix fixe menus.
- **Afternoon:** Stroll the elegant **Palais Royal** and its arcades, and explore a covered passage such as **Galerie Vivienne**.
- **Evening:** Visit the **Opéra Garnier** or head up the **Arc de Triomphe** for city views at sunset.

## Day 2: Museums & Le Marais
- **Morning:** Select between **Musée d’Orsay** (Impressionists) or **Musée de l’Orangerie**; optionally combine both.
- **Lunch/Afternoon:** Explore **Le Marais** with its rich dining options and cultural charm. Visit **Place des Vosges**, then check out **Musée Picasso** or **Musée Carnavalet**.
- **Evening:** Enjoy diverse dining in Le Marais—**Café des Musées** or **Breizh Café** are excellent choices—and end with wine bars like **La Belle Hortense**.

## Day 3: Modern & Quirky Museums + Arcades
- **Morning:** Visit **Musée Rodin** to see iconic sculptures like *The Thinker*, in a beautiful garden setting.
- **Lunch:** Picnic or café near Museum.
- **Afternoon:** Head to the **Musée de la Magie** and **Musée des Automates** for something unique and whimsical.
- **Late Afternoon:** Walk through **Passage du Havre**, one of Paris's modern shopping arcades, for a different arcade experience.
- **Evening:** Dine at a traditional French bouillon such as **Bouillon Chartier** for history and atmosphere (classic ambience, affordable).

## Day 4: Urban Culture & Entertainment
- **Morning:** Explore **Parc Monceau** for a picturesque stroll and charming architecture.
- **Lunch:** Near Parc Monceau; a local café.
- **Afternoon:** Visit a gaming arcade/bar—**Meltdown** (video-game and eSports bar chain with consoles and cocktails).
- **Evening:** Consider an offbeat dining and show experience like the **Moulin Rouge**—a spectacular cabaret dinner and can-can performance.

## Day 5: Wrap-up with Gardens & Culture
- **Morning:** Wander the **Tuileries Garden** and relax by the Grand Bassin.
- **Lunch:** **Café Marly** (if not yet tried) or a nearby bistro.
- **Afternoon:** Visit **Musée du Quai Branly** to explore indigenous art from Africa, Asia, Oceania, and the Americas.
- **Late Afternoon:** Take in final Paris views from the rooftop of **Galeries Lafayette**.
- **Dinner:** Enjoy a farewell meal—maybe revisit a favorite spot or try somewhere new in the city center.
