# 5-Day Paris Trip Plan

## Day 1: Classic Paris & Elegant Dining
- **Morning:** Visit the Louvre and stroll through Jardin des Tuileries, admiring sculptures by Maillol, Rodin, and Giacometti.
- **Afternoon:** Explore Musée d’Orsay or Centre Pompidou for modern and impressionist masterpieces.
- **Evening:** Dinner with a view at Les Ombres (rooftop near the Eiffel Tower) or Ciel de Paris (Tour Montparnasse).

## Day 2: Hidden Funfair Museum & Historic Arcades
- **Morning:** Explore Musée des Arts Forains (Fairground Museum) in the Bercy district (reservation needed).
- **Afternoon:** Wander Passage Brady, a covered arcade filled with South Asian restaurants.
- **Dinner:** Neighborhood bistronomy at Café de l’Usine in Belleville (French classics, modern touch).

## Day 3: Art Immersion & Chic Rooftop Dining
- **Morning:** Visit Pompidou Center for modern art and radical architecture.
- **Lunch/Afternoon:** Mediterranean plates and views at Perruche (Printemps Haussmann rooftop).
- **Evening:** Dine at Radioeat (Asian-fusion and jazz) or Lapérouse (historic Seine-side restaurant).

## Day 4: Insider Culinary Gems & Museum Choices
- **Morning:** Visit Musée Rodin, Musée de l’Orangerie, or a local art museum.
- **Lunch:** Thai–Chinese street-style at Pochana, awarded "Best Little Diner" in Paris (Fooding 2026).
- **Afternoon:** Relax with a cocktail at Lissit bar (11th arrondissement).
- **Evening:** Fine dining at a Michelin-starred restaurant: Le Cinq, Arpège, Épicure, or one of Paris’s new Michelin winners (2025).

## Day 5: Markets, Final Galleries & Farewell Meal
- **Morning:** Explore Marché d’Aligre or Marché des Enfants Rouges for Parisian market atmosphere.
- **Midday:** Optional museum stop (Centre Pompidou or Musée d’Orsay if missed earlier).
- **Afternoon:** Street food or Bib Gourmand value dining (e.g., Mắm From Hanoï for Vietnamese cuisine).
- **Final Dinner:** Upscale meal at Table by Bruno Verjus (2 Michelin stars, #8 World’s 50 Best Restaurants).
