# 5-Day Paris Trip Itinerary

## Day 1 – Iconic Landmarks & Seine River
- **Morning**: Visit the Eiffel Tower and stroll along the Seine.
- **Afternoon**: Enjoy a Seine River cruise for beautiful city vistas. ([itimaker.com](https://www.itimaker.com/blog/paris-itinerary-5-days?utm_source=openai))
- **Evening**: Dinner with a panoramic view at Les Ombres atop Musée du Quai Branly. ([eatlikethefrench.com](https://eatlikethefrench.com/chef-chooses-foodie-things-to-do-in-paris-in-2025/?utm_source=openai))

## Day 2 – Louvre & Montmartre
- **Morning**: Explore the Louvre Museum (check access due to recent strikes). ([livingnomads.com](https://livingnomads.com/2025/05/paris-itinerary-5-days/?utm_source=openai), [reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/frances-louvre-museum-remains-shut-workers-weigh-strike-extension-2025-12-17/?utm_source=openai))
- **Afternoon**: Walk Montmartre, visit Sacré-Cœur and city views.
- **Dinner**: Dine at Mắm From Hanoï for Bib Gourmand-level Vietnamese fare. ([guide.michelin.com](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/article/travel/michelin-best-value-restaurants-paris-2025?utm_source=openai))

## Day 3 – Modern Art & Old Paris
- **Morning**: Visit Musée National d’Art Moderne at Centre Pompidou. ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_National_d%27Art_Moderne?utm_source=openai))
- **Afternoon**: Explore Île de la Cité, see Notre-Dame, and visit Sainte-Chapelle. ([tripscity.com](https://tripscity.com/paris-5-days-itinerary/?utm_source=openai))
- **Dinner**: Rooftop dinner at Perruche or Ciel de Paris. ([eatlikethefrench.com](https://eatlikethefrench.com/chef-chooses-foodie-things-to-do-in-paris-in-2025/?utm_source=openai))

## Day 4 – Culture or Day Trip
- **Option A**: Tour Petit Palais and its garden café. ([gotraveldaily.com](https://www.gotraveldaily.com/top-museums-paris-2025-gotraveldaily/?utm_source=openai))
- **Option B**: Day trip to Château de Fontainebleau for royal history. ([thegeographicalcure.com](https://www.thegeographicalcure.com/post/the-perfect-5-day-paris-itinerary?utm_source=openai))
- **Evening**: Fine dining at Plénitude – Cheval Blanc Paris. ([orangedonuttours.com](https://www.orangedonuttours.com/paris/city-guides/top-10-fine-dining-restaurants-in-paris-2025?utm_source=openai))

## Day 5 – Local Flavor & Museum
- **Morning**: Stroll Canal Saint-Martin, exploring relaxed cafes and shops. ([tripscity.com](https://tripscity.com/paris-5-days-itinerary/?utm_source=openai))
- **Afternoon**: Visit Musée Jacquemart-André and tearoom. ([tripscity.com](https://tripscity.com/paris-5-days-itinerary/?utm_source=openai))
- **Dinner**: Classic café in the Latin Quarter or Saint-Germain-des-Prés.

---

## Practical Tips
- Consider the Paris Museum Pass for value and convenience. ([en.northleg.com](https://en.northleg.com/paris/itineraries/five-days/?utm_source=openai))
- Reserve tickets and restaurants in advance. Watch for pickpockets; check sites for current strike information. ([en.northleg.com](https://en.northleg.com/paris/itineraries/five-days/?utm_source=openai), [reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/opening-paris-louvre-museum-delayed-employees-discuss-possible-strike-2025-12-15/?utm_source=openai))
