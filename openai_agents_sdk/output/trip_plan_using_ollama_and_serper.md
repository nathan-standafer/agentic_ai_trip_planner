>## Notes & Tips
>1. **Travel Insurance**: Highly recommended for international travel.
>2. **Japan Rail Pass**: Consider a 7‑day JR Pass for Kyoto/Osaka travel; savings could be ~¥40 000 per person.
>3. **Language**: Basic Japanese phrases helpful; many signs have English.
>4. **SIM Card**: Purchase a local SIM for navigation and communication.
>5. **Cash**: Japanese ATMs accept foreign cards; keep cash on hand for small vendors.
>6. **Dress Code**: Tea ceremony requires modest attire; no shorts or hats.
>7. **Tokyo Marathon**: Arrive early for race start; dinner reservation at *Kagari* recommended 3 hours before race end.
